PRIVACY POLICY

Widget Design · Last updated: November 5, 2026

TRESO: Widget Design is an app developed by Estudio Treso, a brand of TRESO SPA (Santiago, Chile).

DATA WE COLLECT

None. The app requires no sign-up, has no accounts of its own, shows no ads, uses no analytics services and sends no information to us. We have no servers that receive your data.

DATA THE APP READS ON YOUR PHONE

To show it in your widgets, the app reads on your own phone the time, date, battery and free storage (no permission needed). Only if you grant permission, it also reads: your calendar (events for today and the next 7 days; on iPhone also your reminders), your activity (steps; on iPhone also distance, floors and sleep hours from Health) and your approximate location. Everything is processed on the phone and we never receive it.

WEATHER AND LOCATION

For the weather, the app uses your approximate location. On Android, the location is rounded to about 1 km and sent to the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway, api.met.no) to get the forecast; no other data is sent. Weather data is from MET Norway, licensed under CC BY 4.0. On iPhone, weather is provided by Apple Weather, under Apple’s privacy policy. Sunset time is calculated on the phone.

IN-APP PURCHASES

The Terminal style is free. The other styles and Pack 1 are one-time purchases. Payment is processed by the store (Google Play or the App Store) with your store account, under the store’s own privacy policy. We never receive your payment details: the app only asks the store what you bought in order to unlock your styles.

DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

The app stores on your phone your settings (language, time format, colors, the data chosen for each piece), your note, your countdown and the latest data read so widgets show up instantly. None of it leaves the device, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

PERMISSIONS

Every permission is optional: if you don’t grant it, that item shows as “no permission” and everything else works the same. You can remove them at any time in your phone settings.

CHILDREN

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this same page with its date.

CONTACT

For any privacy question, write to us at contacto@treso.cl
