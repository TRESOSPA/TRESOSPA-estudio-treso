FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How do I add the widgets on Android?
Press and hold an empty spot on your Home screen, tap “Widgets” and find TRESO. Build the kit with “TRESO · Wide” on top, two “TRESO · Square” in the middle and “TRESO · Large” below. Or use “TRESO · Full screen”, the whole kit in one widget. Widgets show the style you picked with “Use style”.
How do I add the widgets on iPhone?
Press and hold the Home Screen, tap “Edit” › “Add Widget” and find TRESO. Add the four kit sizes in the order the app shows (Styles tab › Use style › How to add it).
How do I change a widget’s style or piece?
Press and hold the widget. On Android tap “Settings” (or the pencil or gear icon); on iPhone tap “Edit Widget”. There you choose the style and which of the 4 pieces it shows. To change them all at once, tap “Use style” on the style you want in the app.
The widget landed on another page (Android).
Some phones, Samsung for example, place a new widget wherever there is room, not always on the page you were looking at. Press and hold it and drag it to the page you want.
It says “NO PERMISSION”.
That item needs a permission (calendar, location or activity). On Android, tap the widget and then “Allow”. You can also grant it in the app, under Settings › Permissions, or in your phone settings.
The weather doesn't show up.
Weather needs the location permission and an internet connection. It refreshes every 30 minutes; open the app once to fetch it right away.
What is free and what do I pay for?
The Terminal style is free, with every feature. Each of the other 7 styles is US$0.99, or all 7 together with Pack 1 for US$4.99. If you already bought a style, Pack 1 costs less. It is a one-time payment, with no ads or subscriptions.
I paid and the style is still locked.
Open the app with an internet connection and tap “Restore purchases” in Settings. Use the same Google or Apple account you bought it with. If the payment was pending, the style unlocks once the store confirms it.
The widget clock lags a few seconds (Android).
That is normal: Android refreshes widgets once a minute and may take a few seconds to change the number.
