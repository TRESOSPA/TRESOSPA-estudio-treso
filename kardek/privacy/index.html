PRIVACY POLICY

My Kardek · Last updated: November 16, 2026

My Kardek is an app developed by Estudio Treso, a brand of TRESO SPA (Santiago, Chile).

DATA WE COLLECT

None. The app requires no sign-up, has no accounts, shows no ads, uses no analytics services and sends no information to us. It works offline and we have no servers that receive your data.

DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

Everything you write stays in the app’s private storage on your phone: your cards and their content, your drawers’ names and colors, your settings (language and sounds) and the photos you add. None of it leaves the phone unless you send or save a card as an image. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

PHOTOS

When you add a photo to a card (for example in a tracker or a quote’s logo), you pick it yourself with the camera or the system picker, and the app keeps a copy in its own storage. The app does not browse or read the rest of your gallery. Camera permission is requested only when you tap to take a photo.

DRAWER CODES

If you lock a drawer with a 4-digit code, the app does not store the code: it stores on your phone only a fingerprint of it in order to check it. The code prevents the drawer from being opened in the app, but it does not encrypt its contents. If you forget it, it cannot be recovered; the only way to remove it is to empty that drawer.

SENDING OR SAVING A CARD

When you tap SEND, the app creates the card’s image on your phone. If you choose to send it, the system share menu opens and you decide who gets it and how; whatever you send to another app is subject to that app’s privacy policy. If you choose to save it, on Android it goes to your gallery, in the Pictures › My Kardek folder; on iPhone the system menu opens, where you choose “Save Image”.

SOUNDS AND VIBRATION

They are used only as effects when opening and closing drawers and cards. Sounds can be turned off in Settings.

APP PURCHASE

The purchase is processed by the store (Google Play or the App Store) with your store account, under the store’s own privacy policy. We never receive your payment details.

CHILDREN

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this same page with its date.

CONTACT

For any privacy question, write to us at contacto@treso.cl
