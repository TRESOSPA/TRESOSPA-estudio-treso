FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What’s free and what’s paid?
The app is free with the notebook page: lines, grid, dots or blank. The other 47 templates unlock together with a single payment, forever: no subscription, no ads. Drawer codes, search and sending as JPG or PDF are free.
I changed phones or reinstalled the app and the locks are back.
Open Settings and tap “RESTORE PURCHASE” with the same Google Play (or Apple) account you bought with. The templates come back without paying again. Your cards, however, don’t move on their own: they stay on the old phone.
How do I create a card?
Open a drawer and tap “+ CARD”. Swipe sideways to see the templates in full, or tap a family at the top to jump to it. Tap the template in the middle or “CREATE”.
How do I rename a card?
With the card open, tap its name at the top (next to the pencil) or the card’s own tab. That is the name that shows in the drawer.
How do I change a drawer’s name or color?
On the card file screen tap “EDIT DRAWERS”. Choose the drawer (01 to 04), type the name and tap a label color.
How do I lock or unlock a drawer?
In “EDIT DRAWERS”, choose the drawer and tap “LOCK WITH A CODE”: enter 4 digits and repeat them. To remove it, tap “REMOVE LOCK” and enter the current code. To change it, “CHANGE CODE”.
I forgot the code.
The code cannot be recovered, not even by us. On the code screen tap “I FORGOT THE CODE”: the only way to remove it is to empty that drawer, and its cards are deleted for good.
How do I send or save a card?
With the card open, tap “SEND”. At the top choose the format, “JPG · IMAGE” or “PDF”, then “SEND” to send it by WhatsApp, email or another app, or “SAVE TO PHONE”. On Android the image goes to your gallery (Pictures › My Kardek) and the PDF to Downloads › My Kardek; on iPhone choose “Save Image” or “Save to Files” in the menu that appears. The card comes out edge to edge, without the app’s buttons.
Do quotes calculate the total and tax?
No, on purpose: every country and business calculates differently. You type the amounts, subtotal, tax and total yourself. They are a quick draft to send to a client, not an invoice.
What are budgets for?
To keep track of a party, a trip or the month: you jot down each expense, tick what you have paid and write the total spent and what is left. They don’t calculate on their own either; you type the amounts.
How do I move, duplicate or delete a card?
With the card open, tap “⋯”. There you can move it to another drawer, duplicate it or delete it. If you delete it, you have a few seconds to tap “UNDO”.
The keyboard covers what I type.
The card scrolls up to what you are typing on its own, and you can also move it with your finger. When the keyboard closes it goes back into place.
How do I turn off sounds or change the language?
In Settings (the gear on the card file screen): SOUNDS YES / NO and LANGUAGE ESPAÑOL / ENGLISH. Templates switch language; what you wrote does not.
Where are my cards stored?
Only on your phone. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, they are lost and we keep no copy. To keep an important card outside the app, save it as an image.
Do I need an internet connection?
No. My Kardek works fully offline.
