YOUR NOTE CARD FILE

A real card file on your phone: four drawers you open and close, and cards you take out, write on and put back.

Start with a blank notebook page or pick one of 48 templates: lists, planners, photo trackers, recipes, notes for others, quotes and budgets. Everything is editable, everything stays on your phone and it works offline.

The app is free with the notebook page (lines, grid, dots or blank). The other 47 templates unlock with a single payment, forever.

4 drawersGive each one a name and a label color. Cards sort themselves by name.
48 templatesThe notebook page is free; the other 47, in 10 families, with a one-time payment. Every title, box and option is editable.
Photo trackersA growing plant, a building project, your kids: photo, date and note, one after another.
Quick quotesYour logo, who it’s from, the client and the items. No automatic math: you type your own amounts and taxes.
BudgetsA party, a trip or the month: jot down what you spend, tick what’s paid and keep the total.
