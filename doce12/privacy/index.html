PRIVACY POLICY

12/12 · Last updated: November 16, 2026

12/12: Grow Diary is an app developed by Estudio Treso, a brand of TRESO SPA (Santiago, Chile).

DATA WE COLLECT

None. The app requires no sign-up, has no accounts, shows no ads, uses no analytics services and sends no information to us. It works offline and we have no servers that receive your data.

DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

Everything you log stays in the app’s private storage on your phone: your projects (name, size, lights, plants), the logbook, reminders, your nutrient list, your settings, your light meter calibration and the photos you add. None of it leaves the phone unless you share a report. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

PHOTOS

When you add a photo, you pick it yourself with the system picker or the camera, and the app keeps a copy in its own storage. The app does not browse or read the rest of your gallery.

LIGHT SENSOR AND PHONE MODEL

The meter reads your phone’s ambient light sensor only while that screen is open. The app also reads your phone model and the sensor name to apply a factory calibration when one exists for that model. Everything is processed on the phone and we never receive it.

NOTIFICATIONS

Reminders use local notifications scheduled on your own phone. The permission is optional: if you don’t grant it, everything else works the same. You can remove it at any time in your phone settings.

SHARING A REPORT

If you choose to share or save a grow report, the app uses your phone’s share menu. Whatever you send to another app is subject to that app’s privacy policy.

APP PURCHASE

The purchase is processed by the store (Google Play or the App Store) with your store account, under the store’s own privacy policy. We never receive your payment details.

AGE

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over and collects no data from anyone.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this same page with its date.

CONTACT

For any privacy question, write to us at contacto@treso.cl
