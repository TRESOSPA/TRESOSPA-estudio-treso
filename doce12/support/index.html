FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What does 12/12 mean?
It is the 12 hours on, 12 hours off light schedule used to switch photoperiod plants into flowering. Autoflowers bloom on their own, without changing the schedule.
How accurate is the light meter?
It is approximate. The phone measures lux and the app converts it to PPFD by lamp type, and every phone model reads differently. To fine-tune it, measure at the same spot with a real meter (or a reference app) and use “Calibrate with another meter” on the Measure light screen.
How do I measure properly?
Hold the phone flat, screen facing the lamp, at the height of the plant tops, and keep it still for a few seconds. The sensor is usually above the screen, near the front camera: don’t cover it with your finger. On Android, don’t put paper over the sensor.
My grow has already started. Can I add it?
Yes. When creating the project choose “Already started” and enter the start date. If it is already on 12/12, the app asks since when so it can work out progress and harvest.
How do I switch to flowering?
In the project tap “Switch to flowering”. You can also log the schedule change to 12/12. The estimated harvest date updates on its own, and you can also change it by hand.
How do I move or edit plants?
In the project, open the project settings and tap “Edit project”. Changes are noted in the logbook.
I don't get reminders.
Check that the app has notification permission. Some phones, Samsung for example, stop apps to save battery: in your phone settings, remove 12/12 from sleeping apps or battery saving.
I can't add photos.
Update the app to the latest version. If it keeps happening, write to us with your phone model.
Where is my data stored?
Only on your phone. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is lost. We are working on a backup option for an upcoming version.
How do I change the color or the language?
In Settings. In automatic mode the app is light while your tent lights are on and dark while they are off; you can also keep it always light or dark. Switch the language with ES / EN at the top.
