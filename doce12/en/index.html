GROW DIARY FOR INDOOR TENTS

Follow your indoor grow day by day. Set up your tent with its size, lights and plants, and log waterings, pH, EC, climate, training and photos in a logbook sorted by week.

The app tracks cycle progress, shows how long until lights off, estimates harvest and measures light with your phone’s sensor. At the end you get a report with charts and photos of the whole grow.

WHAT IT DOES

Your tent to scaleSize, medium, lamps and plants you move and arrange just like in your real tent.
LogbookWatering with pH and EC, climate with VPD, light, schedule, stage, training, transplants, issues and notes, with photos.
Light clock18/6, 12/12 or your own schedule, with a countdown to lights off. The app goes dark when your tent does.
Progress and harvestCycle percentage, days in flower and an estimated harvest date you can adjust.
Light meterApproximate PPFD and DLI from your phone’s sensor, by lamp type, with calibration.
RemindersWatering, pest checks or anything you need, with a notification on your phone.
NutrientsYour own list of products and doses, one tap on each watering, with general tips by stage.
Final reportStages, key dates, pH, EC, climate and light charts, and your photos. Ready to share or save.

The light meter is approximate: a phone does not replace a professional PAR meter. The app’s ranges and tips are general references. For ages 18+; follow your country’s laws on growing.
