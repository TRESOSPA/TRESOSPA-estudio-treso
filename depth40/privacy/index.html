PRIVACY POLICY

Depth 40 · Last updated: November 3, 2026

Depth 40 is a game developed by Estudio Treso, a brand of TRESO SPA (Santiago, Chile).

DATA WE COLLECT

None. Depth 40 requires no sign-up, has no accounts of its own, shows no ads and sends no information to us.

FULL GAME PURCHASE

Missions 1 and 2 are free; the rest are unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase. Payment is processed by the store (Google Play or the App Store) with your store account, under the store’s own privacy policy. We never receive your payment details: the game only asks the store whether the purchase exists in order to open the missions.

DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

The game stores on your own phone the language you chose, your settings (music, effects, vibration, screen color) and the missions you completed. This information never leaves the device, we never receive it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game or use “Reset progress” in Settings.

PERMISSIONS

Depth 40 does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or any other personal data. Vibration is used only as a game effect and can be turned off in Settings.

THIRD PARTIES

The game includes no analytics or advertising services. The only external service is the store’s billing system, used only for the full game purchase.

CHILDREN

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this same page with its date.

CONTACT

For any privacy question, write to us at contacto@treso.cl
