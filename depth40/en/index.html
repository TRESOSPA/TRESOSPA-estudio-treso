DEPTH 40

SUBMARINE STEALTH

You are the captain. From the command console you set the heading, the speed and the depth. Every sound you make can give you away.

Depth 40 is a first-person stealth game set inside a submarine. You don't see the sea: you hear it. Sonar, periscope and instinct are all you have.
