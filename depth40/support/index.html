FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How do I change the language?
On the main menu, tap SETTINGS and choose ENGLISH or ESPAÑOL.
I can't hear anything.
Check that your phone isn't on silent and that music and effects are on in SETTINGS. Sound starts with your first tap on the screen.
How do I see the tutorial again?
In SETTINGS, tap “View tutorial”. Each mission briefing also has a VIEW TUTORIAL button.
I lost my progress.
Progress is saved only on your phone. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, it is lost. “Reset progress” in SETTINGS also erases it.
What is free and what do I pay for?
Missions 1 and 2 and the tutorial are free. Missions 3 to 8 are unlocked with a one-time payment, with no ads or subscriptions.
I paid and the missions are still locked.
Open the game with an internet connection, go to MISSIONS › UNLOCK and tap RESTORE PURCHASE. Use the same Google account you bought it with. If the payment was pending, it will open once the store confirms it.
Do I need an internet connection?
Only to buy or restore the purchase. Depth 40 plays fully offline.
I keep getting detected. Any tips?
Go slower, dive deeper and use SILENT mode when they hunt you. Under an ice floe or below the 100 m layer, enemy sonar can barely see you.
